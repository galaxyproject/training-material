title: 'Predictive Microbiome Engineering: Shotgun Metagenomics, Microbial Networks
  & AI-Based Bioreactor Analytics'
description: Predictive Microbiome Engineering is an advanced, mentor-based, three-day
  virtual workshop exploring the integration of artificial intelligence with microbiome
  engineering for sustainable industrial and environmental applications.  Day 1 focuses
  on shotgun metagenomics, microbial community profiling, taxonomic and diversity
  analysis, functional pathways, metabolic guilds, and community resilience. Participants
  will analyze curated metagenomic datasets using Python-based open-source tools and
  MetaPhlAn/HUMAnN output datasets.  Day 2 covers microbial cooperation, competition,
  metabolic cross-feeding, microbial association networks, SPIEC-EASI, synthetic microbial
  consortium design, MICOM, COBRApy, and machine-learning approaches for predicting
  microbial community-bioprocess relationships. Participants will develop a microbial
  interaction network and an AI-based bioprocess prediction workflow.  Day 3 focuses
  on predictive bioreactor modeling, anaerobic digestion, microbial syntrophy, ADM1,
  process stability, and the effects of pH, temperature, substrate conditions, and
  hydraulic retention time. Participants will conduct virtual microbiome and bioreactor
  experiments using Microbe Lab at Live-Labs.org.  Tools covered include Python, Google
  Colab, Pandas, Matplotlib, NetworkX, Scikit-learn, MetaPhlAn/HUMAnN output datasets,
  and Microbe Lab.  The workshop is intended for researchers, students, faculty members,
  and industry professionals working in microbiology, biotechnology, metagenomics,
  microbial ecology, bioinformatics, computational biology, synthetic biology, environmental
  biotechnology, wastewater treatment, anaerobic digestion, bioenergy, and sustainable
  bioprocess research.  Sessions begin at 5:30 PM IST.
