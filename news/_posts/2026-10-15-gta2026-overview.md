# The Galaxy Training Academy 2026 trained researchers in Galaxy data analysis world wide across a variety of scientific topics.

In May 2026, the **Galaxy Training Network (GTN)** hosted the third edition of the **Galaxy Training Academy (GTA)** - a global, self-paced online training event. Counting the GTN Smörgåsbord, this was already the sixt large-scale, open-access learning event organized by the GTN.

The GTA offered this year a total of 11 **training paths**, aka 'tracks'. For the asynchronous learning **pre-recorded video** and detailed **written tutorials**, all free and openly accessible, where supplied for the learners.

In addition the 24h live support by an international team of **dedicated trainers** and **contributors** was hosted on Slack. Starting every day with a daily Icebreaker, the Australian team started every day by welcoming the first learners.


The Galaxy Training Academy began on Monday with a live welcome and introduction session. The first day offered a variety of entry-level training sessions - perfect for those new to Galaxy, with tutorals like 'Galaxy 101'. However also more advanced tutorials where offered including Data manipulation, or High-throughput sequencing (HTS) fundamentals like read mapping and quality control.  

Over the next three days, participants could explore one or more specialized tracks. This year's lineup included both returning favorites and new additions:

 - **Returning Tracks**: Proteomics, Assembly, Transcriptomics, Single Cell, Microbiome, Machine Learning, Variant Analysis, Climate Science, and 'From Zero to Hero with Python' 

 - **New Tracks**: Ecology, Digital Humanities

On Friday, participants had the opportunity to explore additional topics on the GTN, or simply catch up on materials from earlier in the week. 

The full [program]({% link events/2026-05-18-galaxy-academy.md %}#program) and detailed event [setup]({% link events/22026-05-18-galaxy-academy.md %}#setup) instruction were provided to participants in advance to ensure a smooth learning experience.

## Meet the experts 

This year, our new series of Meet the Experts kicked off. Each day of the event, a different expert interview was offered, giving insights into different fields of interest to Galaxy users. The experts covered areas from computational genomics, multi-omics, Genome assembly, single-cell genomics, training, open-source tools, reproducibility, and supporting researchers through Galaxy.

## Participants

This year's GTA saw **2225 registered participants** from across the globe, with particularly strong representation from India and Egypt. Most participants were either **Graduate students** (31.5 %), **Postgraduate students** (28.6 %), or **Undergraduate students** (17.2 %). 

![Image displayed a world map showing the numbers of applicants form each country of the world highlited by the colour (blue) intensity.]({% link /news/images/GTA2025_map.jpg %})

In total 668 certificates where requested, and 402 certificates were issued. The most popular certificate requests was for the Introduction to Galaxy, followed by Microbiome, Proteomics, and Variant analysis Tracks. 



## Community and support

The success of GTA 2025 was made possible by the vibrant GTN community, who contributed to every aspect of the event - from track development and tutorial testing to video recording, tool integration, and user support.

Each track had its own dedicated Slack channel, where trainers and experts provided real-time assistance throughout the week.

It is nice to see that each year trainers of the last years as well as new trainers and helpers join the effort to make the GTA a successful event.

## Feedback

Feedback from participants was overwhelmingly positive. Already during the event, many expressed appreciation for the high-quality content, organization, and support. The majority of participants were satisfied with the event and indicated that they would attend future Galaxy events.
