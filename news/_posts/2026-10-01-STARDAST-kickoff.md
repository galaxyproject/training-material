On September 29 and 30, the **STARDAST** consortium met for the first time to officially kick off the project. STARDAST (STEWARDSHIP AND RECOGNITION FOR DATA SCIENCE TALENT) is a Horizon Europe project bringing together 19 partners from nine countries.

The project aims to develop standardised training curricula and clear career pathways for data stewards, strengthen **FAIR** (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable) and **Open Science** practices, and support competence centres across Europe within the European Open Science Cloud (EOSC) framework. Another key objective is to ensure lasting recognition, appropriate rewards, and career stability for **professionals** working in **data stewardship** and curation.

The Freiburg Galaxy team will contribute its long-standing expertise in FAIR data analysis, research data management, and training. As the host of **usegalaxy.eu** and a key contributor to the **Galaxy Training Network (GTN)**, the team will bring both established infrastructure and extensive knowledge to the project. Together with the **Central Data Facility** of the University of Freiburg, we will lead work on curriculum consolidation and integration, as well as the development of a sustainability strategy.

We are energised by the first hybrid consortium meeting in Heidelberg and look forward to working with all project partners to place data stewards at the heart of science.
