The Activity Bar is always shown on the left of the Galaxy interface. To choose which activities it shows:

1. Click on "**More**" at the bottom of the Activity Bar to open **Additional Activities**
2. Click the {% icon galaxy-star %} star next to an activity to show or hide it in the Activity Bar
3. Click "**Reset**" to restore the default activities
