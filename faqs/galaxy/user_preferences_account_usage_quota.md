1. Log out of Galaxy, then back in again. This refreshes the disk usage calculation displayed in the usage meter in the top bar (summary) and under **Preferences** (exact; click your username in the top bar).

Note: 
- Your account usage quota can be found at the bottom of your user preferences page.
